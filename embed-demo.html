Our advent calendar is here!

This is a pretend blog post that shows how the calendar looks when it's embedded in another website. Every day from December 1 to 24 a new door opens. Click today's door below to see what's behind it.

Below the calendar the blog post simply continues. The calendar grows to its full height, so there are no scrollbars inside it.

Embed it on your own site

Paste this code into any page (WordPress: "Custom HTML" block; Wix: "Embed code"; Squarespace: "Code" block):

<iframe src="https://weiphi.github.io/advent-calendar-2026/?embed=1" title="Advent Calendar 2026" loading="lazy"
        style="width:100%;max-width:900px;border:0;aspect-ratio:3/4"></iframe>
<script src="https://weiphi.github.io/advent-calendar-2026/embed.js" async></script>
